About AI Face Swap

Our free AI face swap tool lets you swap faces in photos right in your browser. Using neural network-based face detection (TinyFaceDetector + 68-point landmark detection), the tool identifies facial features in both source and target images, then warps and blends the source face onto the target with adjustable color matching and feathered edges.

How It Works

  1. AI detects 68 facial landmarks in both images
  2. Source face is scaled and positioned to match target
  3. Skin tone and lighting are color-matched
  4. Feathered mask creates seamless blending
  5. Result is composited onto the target image

Privacy

All processing happens locally in your browser. Photos are never uploaded to any server. The AI model is loaded once from a CDN and cached by your browser for subsequent visits.
